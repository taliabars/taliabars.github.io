About Dog-Eared

Built for readers who know the hardest part of finishing a good book is figuring out what to read next.

Dog-Eared is a book recommendation website designed to make finding your next read easier and more personal. Instead of scrolling through endless lists of books, readers can browse by genre, explore curated reading lists, or find recommendations based on their mood and reading preferences.

Why Dog-Eared?

Finding a great book shouldn't feel like homework. Dog-Eared makes it easier to discover books based on genre, mood, ratings, and other reading preferences, while providing recommendations based on real reading experiences.
